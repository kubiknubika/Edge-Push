## Full description (≤4000)

```
EDGE PUSH is a one-handed, turn-based micro-tactics game of indirect combat.
There are no direct attacks: you shove enemies into chasms, lava, spikes,
barrels, pillars, and into each other.

SEVEN FLOORS. Each is a 5x5 board with 3-5 enemies and never more than two
active threats at once. After floors 1-6 you pick one of three perks. Win by
reaching the end with at least one of your three hearts.

TEN DIFFICULTY LEVELS. From WALK to EDGE. A level changes only the seed pool,
selected from measured difficulty. HP, enemy counts, push rules, perks and
threat activation are identical at every level.

CONTROLS. A D-pad in the lower half of the screen, or a 30px swipe anywhere.
Three large ability buttons with large cooldown digits: hook, throw, wait-with-
shield. Board taps are disabled, so an accidental tap never wastes a turn.

LEARNABLE FROM THE FIRST RUN. A six-step tutorial, a WHO IS WHO legend with
real mini-icons, and a single color contract: cyan = friendly, red = hostile /
denied, amber = object, patterned orange = hazard, bluish-green = safe move.
Color is never the only channel: shapes, labels and hatching duplicate meaning
(WCAG 1.4.1).

AUTOSAVE after every action. Close the game and return to the same run.
Dark neon portrait UI, scaled for small phones, 100dvh with no scrolling.

OFFLINE. No network, account, analytics, ads or purchases. Progress lives only
in localStorage on the device. The INTERNET permission is not requested; the
only permission is VIBRATE for short haptic feedback.

This is not an easy game: an evolved AI without search wins only 18/100 runs,
while a deterministic planner reaches 100% only after the generator was fixed
to reject unwinnable layouts.
```
